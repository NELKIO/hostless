What to try

  • Reload mid-game. Your seat and moves come back from local storage.
  • Kill the network. Moves wait and are delivered when you're back online.
  • Try a VPN. If WebRTC can't connect, the game switches to the relays.
  • Leave after the last move. The other player still gets the result from the relays.
